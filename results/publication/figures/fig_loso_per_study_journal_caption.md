**Figure X. Leave-one-study-out performance across public tissue cohorts.** Bars show macro F1 for each held-out study in the keloid-versus-unaffected-skin endpoint. Evaluation is donor-primary, with sample-level fallback for single-donor or mixed-class-within-donor contrasts as specified by the frozen report. Blue bars denote the seven cohorts in the transferable subset. The orange bar is their unweighted mean LOSO macro F1 (91.8%); the error bar shows the study-level bootstrap 95% confidence interval (84.8–97.7%). GSE173900 is shown for transparency but excluded from the transferable-subset mean because platform diagnostics designated it non-transferable. The subset estimate is therefore conditional and exploratory. The dashed line marks the 75% minimum-fold criterion. LOSO, leave-one-study-out; F1, harmonic mean of precision and recall.
