The central finding

Programs outperform isolated markers when cohorts change.

Fibroblast program features transfer across public tissue cohorts substantially better than singular marker genes.

The transferable subset reaches approximately 0.92 LOSO macro F1. Pooled held-out accuracy is approximately 0.84. Difficult profiles and a known non-transferable platform remain visible rather than being hidden by the average.

Why this can transfer

Different studies measure genes differently. Biological programs preserve more of the shared story.

By comparing groups of related genes within each sample, KeloidBench is less dependent on one platform, one study, or one unusually strong marker.

01 · Program scoring

Within-sample rank contrasts

Genes are mapped to HGNC symbols and ranked inside each sample. Each score is the mean rank of a program’s positive genes minus the mean rank of its reference genes. Minimum-gene coverage is checked before scoring.

sp(x)=mean rank(G+p)−mean rank(G−p)
02 · Learning

Small, transparent linear models

Predefined fibrosis, composition, scar, and fused program views feed standardized linear SVM and regularized logistic models. Donor/accession weighting limits domination by large studies or repeated donors.

03 · Transfer test

Leave one whole study out

One study is held aside. Only the remaining studies select the feature view, model, normalization, and threshold. The top three choices vote, and the untouched study is scored once.

04 · Selective output

A prediction is not permission to guess

A class threshold produces the label. Confidence is max(score, 1 − score); below the fixed confidence policy, the output is abstain. Scores, contributions, coverage, and provenance are retained.

What powers this live page

Frozen four-program linear scorer

The interactive profile panel uses fibroblast, keratinocyte, immune, and endothelial composition signals. Its sigmoid-mapped SVM margin is a research score—not a clinically calibrated probability.

What supports the transfer claim

Separate nested study-held-out ensemble

The broader top-three program ensemble reaches 0.918 mean LOSO macro F1 and 77/92 pooled held-out accuracy on the seven-study transferable subset.

Biological context & provenance

Images explain the problem. Transcriptomes drive the model.

Clinical photography is shown only to ground the phenotype. KeloidBench does not analyze images; it operates on public, de-identified expression profiles.

Use boundary

A research benchmark, not a clinical diagnostic.

Public transcriptomic studies vary in platform, tissue composition, preprocessing, ancestry, and sampling design. Predictions should be interpreted as research-triage outputs, with cohort transferability and abstention reviewed before any biological conclusion.
