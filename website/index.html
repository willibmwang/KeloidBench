The central finding

Programs outperform isolated markers when cohorts change.

Fibroblast program features transfer across public tissue cohorts substantially better than singular marker genes.

The transferable subset reaches approximately 0.92 LOSO macro F1. Pooled held-out accuracy is approximately 0.84. Difficult profiles and a known non-transferable platform remain visible rather than being hidden by the average.

Method architecture

From heterogeneous expression to transferable program evidence.

KeloidBench is a program-grounded, cohort-aware classification system. It compresses each transcriptome into interpretable fibroblast and tissue programs, learns only linear decision functions, and separates prediction from confidence policy and explanation.

01

Input contract

Normalize the vocabulary, then audit coverage.

The scorer accepts a sample-by-gene expression matrix. Gene identifiers are standardized to HGNC symbols, duplicate symbols are collapsed by their mean, non-numeric entries are coerced, and coverage is checked against the versioned program gene sets. The source platform and cohort remain attached as provenance—not as predictive features.

HGNC mappingduplicate collapseprogram QC
02

Program encoder

Replace fragile markers with sample-local biology.

Within each sample, genes are converted to percentile ranks. A program score contrasts the mean rank of its positive genes with the mean rank of its reference genes. This makes the representation depend on relative pathway activity inside a sample rather than on a platform-specific expression scale.

sp(x)=mean rankx(G+p)−mean rankx(G−p)

When a program has no reference set, the positive-gene mean is retained directly. The 16 versioned scores cover ECM/profibrotic activity, remodeling, mechanotransduction, inflammation, fibroblast identity, tissue composition, keratinization, metabolism, neural, and vascular programs. A minimum-gene rule prevents a sparsely represented program from silently appearing informative.

03

Feature architecture

Expose several biological views of the same profile.

The program bank is partitioned into predefined views: fibrosis-only, rank-programs-only, composition-only, scar-discriminative, and a fused multiview. Candidate models operate on these low-dimensional summaries rather than thousands of individual genes, reducing sensitivity to missing probes and cohort-specific marker behavior.

FibrosisCell compositionScar discriminationFused multiview
04

Supervised layer

Fit transparent linear learners with cohort-aware weighting.

Each candidate is a standardized linear model: linear SVM, L2-regularized logistic regression, or elastic-net logistic regression. Donor/accession weights prevent a large study or repeatedly sampled donor from dominating the fit. Inside each training partition, accession-blocked validation selects feature view, learner, normalization, calibration, and decision threshold; the three highest-ranked candidates are averaged as a soft-voting ensemble.

linear SVMridge logisticelastic nettop-3 soft vote
05

Nested evaluation

Make study transfer—not random profile splitting—the test.

In the outer loop, one entire public study is held out. The remaining studies alone perform inner model selection and threshold tuning; the chosen ensemble is then refit and evaluated once on the untouched study. Donor-level summaries and equal-accession fold aggregation keep repeated samples and cohort size from overstating performance.

06

Selective output

Separate model score, class decision, and permission to answer.

The model score is compared with a learned class threshold. Confidence is max(score, 1 − score); when it falls below the fixed policy threshold, KeloidBench returns abstain instead of forcing a label. Every result carries its program scores, linear feature contributions, gene-coverage QC, cohort-transfer status, and source provenance.

A narrative model may summarize this immutable evidence packet, but it is downstream of inference and cannot alter the score, decision, abstention rule, or transfer warning.

Two evidence objects

The interactive scorer and the LOSO claim are deliberately kept separate.

Live profile demo

Frozen Stage-B scorer

A standardized linear SVM fitted on 83 eligible profiles. It uses four composition program contrasts—fibroblast, keratinocyte, immune, and endothelial—with a class threshold of 0.498. The SVM margin is mapped through a sigmoid for the displayed score; it is a research score, not a clinically calibrated probability.

Cross-study evidence

Nested program ensemble

For each outer study, inner accession-blocked validation selects a top-three soft-voting ensemble from the broader program views and linear learners. On the seven-study transferable subset, this separate evaluation yields 0.918 mean LOSO macro F1 and 77/92 pooled held-out accuracy.

Biological context & provenance

Images explain the problem. Transcriptomes drive the model.

Clinical photography is shown only to ground the phenotype. KeloidBench does not analyze images; it operates on public, de-identified expression profiles.

Use boundary

A research benchmark, not a clinical diagnostic.

Public transcriptomic studies vary in platform, tissue composition, preprocessing, ancestry, and sampling design. Predictions should be interpreted as research-triage outputs, with cohort transferability and abstention reviewed before any biological conclusion.
